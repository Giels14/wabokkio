# About us

We are Giels and Marjanne. Together we have travelled to more than sixty countries, and when our daughters came along we simply kept travelling. Booking just got a lot more complicated: which room fits two adults and two little ones, is there a kids club for toddlers, will there be a baby cot, and how long is the transfer with a sleeping two-year-old on your lap?

More and more often, friends asked us for tips. Honestly, we think the research is the best part of the trip. We watch endless vlogs, compare resorts and know exactly what families should and should not look out for. So we turned it into Wabokkio.

At Wabokkio we find the family room that really suits your family, at the rates we can get as a registered travel agent (IATA TIDS 96223061). You tell us who is coming along and what you are looking for, and we come back with a personal proposal. Tested ourselves where possible, with honest advice.

Giels and Marjanne
