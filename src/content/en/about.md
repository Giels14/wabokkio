# About us

We’re Giels and Marjanne. Between us we’ve travelled to more than sixty countries, and when our daughters came along, we simply kept on travelling. Booking, though, became a lot more complicated: which room works for two adults and two little ones? Is there a kids’ club for toddlers? Will a cot be ready for us? And how long is the transfer with a sleeping two-year-old on your lap?

More and more, friends started asking us for tips. And to be honest, we think the research is the most fun part of any trip. We watch vlogs for hours on end, compare resorts and know exactly what families should – and shouldn’t – look out for. So we turned it into Wabokkio.

At Wabokkio, we find the family room that really suits your family, at the rates we can access as a registered travel agent (IATA TIDS 96223061). Tell us who’s coming and what you’re looking for, and we’ll come back to you with a personal proposal. Tried and tested by us wherever possible, with honest advice.

Giels and Marjanne
