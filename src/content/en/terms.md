1. **Wabokkio**: Wabokkio B.V., trading as Wabokkio, Groot Emiclaer 157, 3822 TL Amersfoort, the Netherlands, Chamber of Commerce (KvK) 58114262, IATA TIDS 96223061.
2. **Customer**: the person who makes a request or booking, also on behalf of their fellow travellers.
3. **Provider**: the hotel, resort or accommodation that provides the stay, and where applicable the booking platform through which Wabokkio books.
4. **Booking**: the agreement between the customer and the provider about a stay, concluded through the mediation of Wabokkio.

## Article 2. Applicability
1. These terms apply to all requests, quotes and bookings via Wabokkio.
2. The provider's terms also apply, such as the cancellation and payment terms. Wabokkio shares these with the customer before the booking.

## Article 3. Role of Wabokkio
1. Wabokkio is an intermediary. The agreement about the stay is concluded between the customer and the provider.
2. Wabokkio does not offer package holidays and does not sell combinations of accommodation with transport.

## Article 4. Request and quote
1. A request via the website is without obligation.
2. A quote is valid for the period stated in it. Prices and availability may change until confirmation.
3. Wabokkio sends a quote within 2 working days.

## Article 5. Booking and confirmation
1. A booking is final when the customer has accepted the quote in writing, has paid any deposit and Wabokkio has confirmed the booking.
2. The customer checks the confirmation immediately and reports errors within 24 hours.
3. The customer ensures correct details of all travellers, including the ages of children on arrival.

## Article 6. Price and payment
1. The price in the quote includes VAT and mandatory costs, unless stated otherwise. Local tourist tax may have to be paid on site.
2. The payment terms are stated in the quote. In case of late payment, the booking may be cancelled in accordance with the provider's terms.
3. Wabokkio does not charge a service fee.

## Article 7. Changes and cancellations
1. Changes and cancellations are subject to the provider's terms as stated in the quote.
2. A change by the customer is free of charge, unless the hotel charges costs. Those costs are passed on one-to-one.
3. The 14-day right of withdrawal does not apply to accommodation on a specific date (article 6:230p sub e of the Dutch Civil Code).
4. We recommend cancellation and travel insurance.

## Article 8. Liability
1. The provider is responsible for the performance of the stay.
2. Wabokkio is only liable for errors in the mediation itself. This liability is limited to the amount Wabokkio has received for the mediation, except in case of intent or gross negligence.
3. Wabokkio passes on information from providers, such as photos and facilities, in good faith. Wabokkio is not liable for it.

## Article 9. Complaints
1. Report a complaint about the stay immediately on site to the provider, so they can resolve it.
2. If that does not work, report the complaint as soon as possible and no later than one month after your return to travel@wabokkio.nl. Wabokkio will help you resolve it with the provider.

## Article 10. Privacy
Wabokkio processes personal data in accordance with the privacy policy on wabokkio.nl.
