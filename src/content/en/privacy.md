## Who we are
Wabokkio is a trade name of Wabokkio B.V., Groot Emiclaer 157, 3822 TL Amersfoort, the Netherlands, Chamber of Commerce (KvK) 58114262. We are responsible for processing your personal data as described in this policy. Contact: travel@wabokkio.nl, +31 6 43779858.

## What data we process
- Name, email address and phone number.
- Travel details: destination, travel dates, number of adults and children, and the ages of the children.
- For a booking: the names of all travellers as required by the hotel, and any special requests (for example a baby cot or dietary requirement).
- Technical data of your visit to the website, such as your IP address, for security purposes only.

## Why we use this data
- To answer your request and prepare a quote (performance of a contract or steps prior to it).
- To make a booking with the hotel or our booking partners (performance of the contract).
- To comply with legal obligations, such as the tax retention obligation.
- To secure the website (legitimate interest).

We will not send you newsletters or advertising without your explicit consent.

## Children's data
We only ask for the ages of children, because hotels base prices and rooms on them. We only ask for children's names for a confirmed booking, when the hotel needs them.

## Who we share data with
- Hotels and accommodations where you book.
- Booking platforms for travel agents that we use, such as RateHawk and Expedia TAAP.
- Our IT suppliers for hosting and email (including Cloudflare, Resend, Hostnet and Google). We have data processing agreements with them.

Some parties are located outside the European Economic Area. In that case we ensure appropriate safeguards, such as the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission. We never sell your data.

## How long we keep data
- Requests without a booking: no longer than 12 months.
- Booking data: 7 years, due to the tax retention obligation.

## Security
The website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We store requests encrypted and only we have access to them.

## Cookies
We only use functional cookies that are necessary for the website to work. If we start using analytics or marketing cookies in the future, we will ask for your consent first.

## Your rights
You may access, correct or delete your data, restrict processing, object and have your data transferred. Send an email to travel@wabokkio.nl. We respond within one month. If you disagree with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

## Changes
This policy may change. The most recent version is always available on wabokkio.nl.
