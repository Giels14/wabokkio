# Beach holiday with a toddler: sand, sea and keeping your cool

A toddler on the beach is magic: first ripples, buckets, wet trousers from pure joy. It’s also sand in the sandwich and “where did that one sandal go?”. A few steady routines tip the day toward fun.

## Shade first, then play

Set up the umbrella, beach tent or shade cloth first. Only then swimwear, hat and play. For babies under six months, shade is the main protection; for toddlers a shady base remains the place to eat, nap and cool down. Pick a spot near toilets or an exit — toddlers have timing.

## Go early or late

Harsh midday hours are tough on little bodies. Early morning or late afternoon means softer light, cooler sand and often a quieter beach. Midday can be a nap back at the room. Really: that’s not a wasted holiday day, that’s strategy.

## Gear that works on sand

A regular stroller sinks. Smarter: a carrier for the walk across the sand, or a beach wagon for bags and child. Water shoes protect against hot stones. Swim nappies leak — pack a regular nappy for the ride back. A mesh bag or wet bag shakes sand out without drama.

## Food and drink along

Toddlers forget to drink when they’re digging. Bring water and offer it often. A small cooler with fruit, cheese and familiar snacks prevents hangry moments far from the snack bar. Eat a solid meal before you head to the beach; light snacks there keep you going.

## Boundaries in the sand

Mark a “home base” with towels or bags. Stay within arm’s reach of the water — ripples are fun but unpredictable. A properly fitting life jacket (not floaties alone) can help older toddlers who are already bold.

## Pack the night before

Lay out swimwear, fill bottles, check sunscreen: in the morning you only grab the bag. Less chaos, more playtime.
