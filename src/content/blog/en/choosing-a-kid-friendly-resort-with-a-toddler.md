# Choosing a kid-friendly resort with a toddler: what to look for

“Family friendly” on the website sounds lovely. Until you arrive and discover the kids’ club starts at six, the “shallow pool” comes up to your waist, and the room is a half-hour walk from everything. With a toddler you want something else: short distances, calm corners and low friction.

## Short walks always win

Pick a compact resort or a place where room, breakfast, pool and beach sit close together. Every extra metre with a stroller, beach bag and sleeping toddler costs energy you’d rather spend on ice cream. A short airport transfer often matters more than half an extra star.

## Sleep first, splash later

Check that a cot is available — and reserved, not “maybe on the day”. Blackout curtains, a quieter wing and a lift or ground floor make bedtime kinder. A mini-fridge for milk and snacks is gold.

## Water on a toddler scale

Ask how deep the kids’ pool really is. A splash pad or very shallow basin is often better for toddlers than a big “kids pool” where older children dive. Shade by the water isn’t a luxury: an umbrella or covered area saves sunburn and meltdowns.

## Food without the circus

Flexible buffet or child-friendly hours, high chairs and the option of simple late snacks help a lot. All-inclusive can be lovely with a toddler — less hauling bags around — as long as the grounds aren’t huge and there are enough quiet corners.

## Kids’ club? Check the age

Many clubs only start at three, four or even five. Good to know before you book so expectations stay honest. For toddlers what counts most is a play corner, shallow water and enough room to nap in your room.

## Ask concrete questions

Get answers in writing about the cot, pool depth, distance to the beach, laundry and whether balconies are child-safe. Vague replies (“that usually works out”) are a cue to dig deeper or choose differently.
