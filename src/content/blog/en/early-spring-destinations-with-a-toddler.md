# Early spring with a toddler: destinations that actually work

High summer with a toddler: heat, crowds, long queues for ice cream. Early spring — think March to May — is often the quiet winner. Milder weather, shorter waits, and you can still be outside in daytime without everyone melting.

## Why shoulder season feels good

Toddlers love predictability and space to play. Fewer tourists mean calmer beaches, a table sooner, and hotels that still have real time for a cot. Flights and stays are often kinder on the budget than in July — handy when you plan on request and want a quote first.

## Southern Europe, lighter

Think short flights from the Netherlands: for example the Algarve, parts of Spain or the Balearics in April–May. The sea can still be cool, but sun, playgrounds and child-friendly restaurants are plentiful. Choose a base with flat paths, a shallow pool or splash corner, and shade — spring sun is stronger than you think.

## City + green instead of museums only

Cities with parks and playgrounds (and stroller-friendly stretches) work better than a schedule packed with museums. One morning outing, afternoon rest: that rhythm fits toddlers, whether you lean toward a southern coast or a greener northern European city. Check a rain plan: indoor soft play, a covered pool, or simply the room with toys.

## What you always check

- Flight length and whether you can go direct.  
- Transfer time to the stay.  
- Weather: pack layers.  
- Whether beach or pool is open and pleasant that month.  
- Cot, blackout, fridge.

## One base, no hopping

Prefer five to seven nights in one place over three hotels in a week. Toddlers bloom once the bedroom feels familiar. Day trips from a fixed base beat pack-unpack cycles.
